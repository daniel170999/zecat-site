what this is

a memecoin that settles on a chain where nobody can see how your week went.

how to buy

four things to understand before you send anything.

this chain is slower and stricter than what you are used to. none of it is broken, it just works differently. read the four parts once and nothing will surprise you.

01

funding

Zcash has two address types. shielded addresses start with u1, transparent ones start with t1. money moves freely between them, there is nothing to memorise.

shld.fun settles to shielded addresses only, so you need a wallet that supports u1:

  • zodl recommended by the platform. its built-in swap bridges SOL, EVM chains and more straight into Zcash.
  • zingo
  • ywallet
  • zecwallet
  • noir
  • nighthawk

once the ZEC lands, shield it before you trade.

02

trading

create a trading wallet on the platform and save the seed phrase offline. every token you buy lives in that wallet and never leaves it. lose the phrase, lose the bag. nobody can recover it for you.

buying. set a refund address first, shielded or transparent both work. the platform then generates a deposit address and tells you the exact amount to send. send exactly that amount. after three to five confirmations the token shows up in your portfolio on shld.fun.

selling. enter a receiver address and your ZEC arrives there once the order fills. shielded or transparent, both work.

zodl generates a new address after every transaction. old and new belong to the same wallet tree, so nothing is lost if you misplace one.

03

slippage

orders settle a few blocks after they are quoted, so on busy blocks the price moves in between. a fill at a worse price than the quote is normal market behaviour here, not a bug. size your trades with that in mind.

orders are all or nothing. they fill completely or they get refunded. there is no such thing as a partial fill.

every fill is a whole number of lots, and one lot is 12,500 tokens. that is why the amounts look the way they do.

04

patience

depending on how busy the network is, a single trade can take anywhere from five minutes to two hours. this is the original privacy chain and the wait is part of it.

if your trade has not appeared yet, it is almost certainly still waiting for blocks. check your portfolio on shld.fun before assuming anything went wrong.
