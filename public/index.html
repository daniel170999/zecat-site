questions

the ones the telegram asks every day.

what is $ZECAT

a meme token on Zcash, traded through shld.fun. one billion of them. the first token ever launched on that platform. it holds the lowest issue block there and anyone can check that on chain.

what is shld.fun, is it part of Zcash

a market for shielded tokens built on Zcash. it is an independent project. not Electric Coin Co. and not the Zcash Foundation. experimental tech, interact at your own risk.

how do i buy it

get shielded ZEC in a wallet, open shld.fun, pick $ZECAT, send ZEC to the address it gives you. the five steps are further up this page.

why does my trade take a minute to show up

a Zcash block lands about every 75 seconds. your order settles on layer 1 and waits for one. that is the whole delay.

is this actually private

pay from a shielded balance and that buy can stay off the public ledger. pay from a transparent address and that link is on chain forever. reused addresses, exchange withdrawals and timing are not shielded either.

why do orders fill in lots of 12,500

the supply is cut into 80,000 units. one unit is 12,500 tokens. every fill is a whole number of units, so every trade you see is a multiple of 12,500.

what does graduated mean

the token started on a bonding curve and moved to a liquidity pool once enough was sold. $ZECAT did that on 3 september, nineteen minutes after its first trade.

what is the deal with 75 seconds

it is the Zcash block time. in this world it is a gold bell that rings, everything pauses a beat, then carries on. 1,152 of them in a day. the cat counts in bells.
