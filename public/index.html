What this is

A memecoin on a chain that keeps your balance to itself.

Shielded by default

shld.fun settles to shielded Zcash addresses only. Fund a buy from a shielded balance and it stays off the public ledger. Fund it from a transparent address and that link is on chain.

Not a new team

shld.fun is built by the people behind Zecscriptions, the ZRC-20 platform that has run on Zcash since before it. Same team, same chain, different mechanism.

How to buy

Four things to know before you send anything.

Zcash is slower and stricter than most chains. None of this is broken, it just works differently.

  1. 01

    Fund a shielded wallet

    Zcash has two address types. Shielded addresses start with u1, transparent ones start with t1. Money moves freely between them.

    shld.fun settles to shielded addresses only, so you need a wallet that supports u1.

    • ZodlRecommended by the platform. Its built-in swap bridges SOL, EVM chains and more straight into Zcash.
    • Zingo
    • Ywallet
    • Zecwallet
    • Noir
    • Nighthawk

    Once the ZEC lands, shield it before you trade.

  2. 02

    Create a trading wallet

    The platform generates a wallet for you. Save the seed phrase offline. Every token you buy lives in that wallet and never leaves it. Lose the phrase and the bag is gone, because nobody can recover it for you.

    Zodl generates a new address after every transaction. Old and new belong to the same wallet tree, so nothing is lost if you misplace one.

  3. 03

    Buy, then sell

    Buying. Set a refund address first, shielded or transparent. The platform gives you a deposit address and an exact amount. Send exactly that amount. After three to five confirmations the token appears in your portfolio.

    Selling. Enter a receiver address. Your ZEC arrives there once the order fills.

    Orders fill in whole lots of 12,500 tokens. That is why the amounts look the way they do.

  4. 04

    Wait it out

    Orders settle a few blocks after they are quoted, so the price can move in between. A fill worse than the quote is normal here. Orders are all or nothing: they fill completely or they refund, never partially.

    A single trade can take five minutes to two hours depending on how busy the network is.

    If a trade has not appeared yet, it is almost certainly still waiting for blocks. Check your portfolio before assuming anything went wrong.
